# WealthBuilder

A personal crypto bank for long-term crypto wealth building.

WealthBuilder is being built for people who want more of their financial life to live on-chain without having to manually manage every wallet, protocol, risk decision, and portfolio action themselves.

The product starts with long-term crypto wealth management and is designed to expand over time into a broader crypto-native financial experience.

## Core Idea

WealthBuilder revolves around a **Personal Wealth Policy**.

Users define things such as:

- long-term financial goals
- risk preferences
- asset preferences
- liquidity requirements
- protocol exposure limits
- level of AI autonomy

WealthBuilder then uses those rules to monitor, recommend, and eventually execute financial actions within user-defined boundaries.

## Product Principles

- **Self-custodial** — users remain in control of their assets
- **User-defined strategy** — decisions are guided by the user's goals and risk preferences
- **Bounded AI autonomy** — automation operates only within explicitly granted permissions
- **Security-aware** — protocol and exposure risk are considered alongside potential returns
- **Long-term focused** — designed around building sustainable crypto wealth rather than short-term yield chasing

## Product Direction

WealthBuilder begins with:

**Grow**  
Long-term crypto wealth management.

Over time, the broader vision is to support more of the user's financial life:

**Grow → Borrow → Spend**

The goal is a crypto-native financial home where users can build, manage, and eventually use their wealth without giving up control.

## Current Status

WealthBuilder is an early-stage project currently preparing for closed beta.

This repository contains the public landing page and early-access experience.

The core application is being developed separately.
